# rgeomex: Geographic Boundaries of Mexico from INEGI and INE

Polygons of Mexico from INEGI 2019 and INE 2020 at several resolutions
(states, municipalities, localities, urban AGEB, blocks and electoral
sections), and functions to extract the AGEB, blocks and sections of a
locality. The datasets are GeoParquet files that are downloaded the
first time they are used and kept in a local cache.
