# rgeomex: Geographic Boundaries of Mexico from INEGI and INE

Geographic boundaries of Mexico (states, municipalities, localities,
AGEB, blocks and electoral sections) from INEGI 2019 and INE 2020, and
functions to extract the AGEB, blocks and electoral sections of a
locality.
